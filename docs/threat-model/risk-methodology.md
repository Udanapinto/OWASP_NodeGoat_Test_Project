# Threat Risk Assessment Methodology

## Owner

Member 2

## Method

The project uses a simple 3 × 3 qualitative risk matrix.

Risk Score:

Likelihood × Impact

## Likelihood

| Value | Rating | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Low | Difficult or uncommon in the current environment |
| 2 | Medium | Realistic but requires specific conditions |
| 3 | High | Relatively straightforward against the vulnerable application |

## Impact

| Value | Rating | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Low | Limited effect |
| 2 | Medium | Meaningful effect on user/application data |
| 3 | High | Major confidentiality, integrity or availability impact |

## Risk Classification

| Score | Classification |
|---:|---|
| 1–2 | Low |
| 3–4 | Medium |
| 6 | High |
| 9 | Critical |

## Important

The risk ratings describe the intentionally vulnerable local
NodeGoat project environment.

They are not ratings of an external production system.

Threats identified during Phase 7 must later be validated against the
exact pinned application version before being described as confirmed
vulnerabilities.
